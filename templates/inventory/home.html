Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Learn how InvenTrack accelerates warehouse tracking, governance, and reporting.

How does the low-stock alert system work?
Each product has a customizable minimum reorder threshold. Whenever a stock deduction brings the quantity at or below this threshold, InvenTrack immediately flags the product with a warning indicator and generates an urgent restock card on the dashboard.
Can I assign vendors directly to products?
Yes! You can register multiple suppliers in the Supplier Directory with their phone and email credentials. Products can be linked directly to their primary vendor for streamlined replenishment.
How are user permissions and roles managed?
InvenTrack provides three tiers of authorization: Staff (read & log movements), Managers (create & update catalog products and suppliers), and Administrators (system-wide control including product deletions).
Can I quickly adjust stock without creating full purchase orders?
Yes, every product detail card features a "Quick Stock Adjustment" tool where operators can log incoming units or damage write-offs in just a few clicks.
